Add DryRoute to your phone

Use DryRoute like a regular app.

Add DryRoute to your Home Screen

  1. Open DryRoute in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add

DryRoute will now appear on your Home Screen like a normal app.

Install DryRoute on your phone

  1. Open DryRoute in Chrome
  2. Tap the browser menu
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app
  4. Confirm the installation

DryRoute will now appear alongside your other apps.
